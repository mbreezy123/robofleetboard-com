FAQ

Straight answers

Who are you?

I'm Matt Breen, the founder. RoboFleet Board is a small, new, independent service that I run myself, from New York. You deal with me directly.

What does it cost?

Free for your first batch, then simple flat pricing quoted upfront before you commit. No card needed to start.

What do you need from me?

Whatever list you have of your robots, sites and vendor contacts. A spreadsheet or a few photos is fine. If your robots have an app or logs you can export, those help.

Do you contact my customers?

Not unless you ask me to. The board tells you who to call; the call stays with you and your staff.

How do I opt out?

Reply "no" to any email from me and I won't email you again. You can also ask me to delete anything you've sent by writing to matt@robofleetboard.com, and I will.
